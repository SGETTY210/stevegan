## Ingredients

- 2¼ cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup butter, softened
- ¾ cup sugar
- ¾ cup brown sugar
- 2 eggs
- 2 tsp vanilla
- 2 cups chocolate chips

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F.
2. Whisk together the flour, baking soda and salt.
3. Beat the butter and both sugars until creamy. Add the eggs and vanilla.
4. Mix in the flour mixture, then stir in the chocolate chips.
5. Drop spoonfuls onto a baking sheet and bake for 9–12 minutes.
